TCP Three-Way Handshake

The TCP three-way handshake is the process used to establish a connection between two devices.

It has three stages:

	1.	SYN: The client sends a request to establish a connection.
	2.	SYN-ACK: The server responds by acknowledging the request.
	3.	ACK: The client acknowledges the response, completing the connection.

Why It Matters in Cybersecurity
SOC analysts monitor TCP connections to identify unusual connection attempts and investigate possible attacks such as SYN floods.

Important Note
A SYN packet alone does not prove that an attack is happening. Analysts must examine the traffic pattern and other evidence.
